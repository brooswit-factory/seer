### Fixed
- Edge arrowheads (`marker-end`) were drawn at the target node's own centre — the simulation's raw x/y — so most or all of the arrowhead sat underneath the node shape, which paints on top (`nodeLayer` is appended after `edgeLayer`). `public/app.js`'s `renderPositions` now pulls the edge's end point back to the target node's boundary (new pure helper `public/edge-geometry.js`'s `edgeEndPoint`). Confirmed by serving the viewer locally against the committed fixture graph and comparing cropped screenshots before/after — not inferred from the geometry alone (see `docs/review-evidence/`).
- The boundary itself uses each node's ACTUAL drawn shape, not an equal-area circle: `approxRadiusForNode`'s circle underestimated the true boundary of every non-circular shape (a Bug/triangle's apex, for instance, sits well outside its equal-area circle), still burying the arrowhead tip. New pure helper `public/node-boundary.js`'s `farthestPointRadius` runs the node's real D3 symbol `draw(context, size)` against a recording context and returns the farthest point it actually visits — a conservative enclosing radius that clears the shape from every approach angle, plus half the border stroke's own width for the rendered outline. Verified against representative resource types (Epic/hexagon, Story/square, Task/circle, Bug/triangle, Sub-task/diamond) locally, not just the fixture's untyped rounded-square fallback nodes (see `docs/review-evidence/FACTORY-882-non-circular-shapes.png`).
